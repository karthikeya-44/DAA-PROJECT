                            HOW IT WORKS
                        

                        
Algorithm Explanation

                    


                


                

                    Bubble Sort works by repeatedly comparing
                    neighboring elements. If two neighboring
                    elements are in the wrong order, they are
                    swapped. After each pass, the largest
                    unsorted element moves toward the end.
